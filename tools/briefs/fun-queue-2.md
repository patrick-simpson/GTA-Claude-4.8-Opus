Owner: "PORT CALAMITY: 50 IDEAS TO MAKE IT MORE FUN AND MORE PLAYABLE. Use haiku where you can. Do each of
them one at a time, then push it to the live game."

Rules for this queue (lead):
- ONE item at a time, in order. Each item: worker builds and verifies, the lead checks in a clean copy and
  pushes with a player-facing commit message (the WHAT'S NEW card), then the next item starts.
- Model: Haiku where the job is small and mechanical (text lines, toggles, simple UI); Sonnet otherwise;
  Fable only for visual-quality judgement if needed.
- Anything needing a protocol bump, a server deploy, a new account/cloud service or the owner's personal
  data: stop and ask the owner first (one question).
- Same engineering rules as before (common.md, net=0 tests, Blender visible, battery rule, getX() for
  meshopt attributes, game.flip for per-frame buffers, commit-prep from HEAD into scratch/<item>/commit/).
